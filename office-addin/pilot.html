bibdocqmd / HOSTED PILOT

Quarto-native citations in Word

This is the static pilot host for the bibdocqmd Office add-in.

Install the pilot manifest

Download the bibdocqmd pilot manifest, then upload it through Word's add-in interface.

The pilot reads bibliography files selected in the task pane. It does not upload or store their contents.

Development history, documentation, licensing, and issue tracking are available in the public bibdocqmd repository.
