5. Place the bibliography

Put the cursor where the references must appear. Add one marker for the whole document or one inside each heading section that needs its own references.

First lines align with the left margin. Wrapped lines indent by this amount; use 0 for no indent. Applies to all markers when you choose Update in Word.
Scoped markers use Word Heading 1–3 structure, not page-layout section breaks.
